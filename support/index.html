Twork Support

Help and support for the Twork iOS app

Twork helps trainers manage clients, schedules, training sessions, subscriptions, services and business records.

Need help?

If you experience a problem, please include the Twork app version, your iOS version, the feature you were using, and a short description of what happened when contacting the Twork developer through the official Twork distribution or developer contact channel.

Privacy and your data

Twork is a local-first app. Your app data is kept on your device unless you choose an Apple-provided storage or sharing feature such as iCloud backup or Calendar.

Read the Twork Privacy Policy

Troubleshooting

For unexpected behaviour, make sure you are using the latest available version of Twork and iOS. Restarting the app can also resolve temporary system or permission issues.

Calendar or Contacts access

Features that use Calendar or Contacts require the corresponding iOS permission. You can review these permissions in iOS Settings.
