TRAINER CRM GUIDE · iOS

How to choose a CRM for personal trainers

A workout diary for an athlete is not the same as a customer-management app for a trainer. Compare your working processes before choosing software.

Workout tracker versus trainer CRM

A workout tracker primarily records one person’s exercises and performance. A trainer CRM helps a coach manage appointments, client records, subscriptions, debts and services for multiple paying clients. The right choice starts with the people and processes you manage.

Test the full booking-to-payment workflow

Create a fictional client, schedule a session, move it, mark it completed and inspect the package balance. Check cancellations, financial corrections and histories. If essential steps still require a second spreadsheet, compare other products.

Offline versus cloud

Cloud software can support teamwork and multiple devices but depends on internet and provider infrastructure. Local-first software can keep primary records on an iPhone and support offline work, but you need to understand backups and device replacement.

Bookkeeping is not payment processing

Recording a €50 payment is not the same as charging a card. Check whether a product provides invoices, payment acceptance or only an operational ledger. Trainers may need more than one tool.

Client experience and privacy

A dedicated client portal is not always necessary. Some trainers send schedules through messaging or share a personal calendar. Review who can see appointment notes, debts and other sensitive information.

International and club work

Check how time-zone and daylight-saving changes affect future bookings. For fitness clubs, verify separate personal and club client balances, fee percentages and settlements.

TWORK's actual scope

TWORK is an early-access iPhone-first, local-first CRM for personal, club and online trainers. It includes client management, sessions, packages, payment records and optional Apple Calendar sharing. An Android app, online card processing, built-in video calls and a multi-user cloud CRM are not claimed. Check current availability and independent feedback before choosing.

Common questions

What is the difference between a workout app and CRM?

The first tracks an individual’s exercises; the second helps a trainer organize paying clients and business operations.

Does TWORK accept card payments?

No integrated card-payment gateway is claimed.
