TWORK FEATURES · iOS

Trainer and fitness-club financial settlements

Keep independent clients and club sessions organized while tracking the financial relationship with each fitness club.

One schedule, separate financial contexts

Coaches may work at a club in the morning and train private clients later. TWORK keeps the training workflow connected while distinguishing personal and club-related financial records.

Capture settlement rules and outcomes

Track services, club duties, client-side amounts and coach/club settlements. The app’s calculation flows are designed to keep money-related updates linked to training activity instead of requiring a separate reconciliation spreadsheet.

Review before settling

Club arrangements differ. Confirm the configured percentages, fees, duties and service rules before relying on a total. TWORK is an operational record, not a substitute for a contract or professional accounting.

Common questions

Is this only for gym employees?

No. TWORK is intended for trainers who handle independent clients, club clients, or both.

Does TWORK replace club accounting software?

No. It helps the trainer track their side of sessions and settlements; accounting and contractual obligations remain separate.
