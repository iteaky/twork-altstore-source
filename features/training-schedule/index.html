TWORK FEATURES · iOS

Training schedule and session management

Plan personal, group and online sessions while keeping changes connected to the right client and subscription.

A timetable built around sessions

See upcoming training sessions, track client-specific appointments and work with club duties in the same schedule. The schedule is part of the client record rather than an unrelated calendar list.

Handle cancellations and rescheduling carefully

When a time changes, the schedule and related records need to remain consistent. TWORK includes workflows for training changes and handling time zones; not every edit should silently change a client’s confirmed appointment.

Calendar integration is optional

Use TWORK on its own or enable Apple Calendar synchronization. Review the calendars and data being shared before granting access or sending an invitation.

Common questions

Can I run my schedule without Apple Calendar?

Yes. Apple Calendar integration is optional, and TWORK keeps its primary training records locally.

Can I track sessions in different time zones?

TWORK supports time-zone-aware scheduling; review the time-zone settings when working with international clients.
