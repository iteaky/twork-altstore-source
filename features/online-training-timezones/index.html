TWORK FEATURES · iOS

Online coaching across time zones

Schedule remote clients in different countries while keeping the training time and the trainer’s local view understandable.

Know whose time you are seeing

International coaching requires more than copying a time from chat. TWORK stores client time-zone information and offers time-zone-aware training workflows so the trainer can reason about local and client times.

Daylight saving changes need explicit rules

A future session can shift differently depending on whether the chosen reference is the trainer's or client's wall-clock time. TWORK includes scheduling settings for daylight saving transitions; confirm them before promising clients a time.

Keep operational context with the session

Use client records, training subscriptions and messaging drafts alongside the schedule. The app does not claim to host video calls or automatically deliver all messages for you.

Common questions

Does TWORK automatically host online workouts?

No built-in video-call hosting is advertised. TWORK helps organize online coaching operations.

What happens when daylight saving time starts or ends?

TWORK has time-zone and schedule-change workflows. The result depends on configured rules, so review upcoming sessions when clocks change.
