TWORK FEATURES · iOS

Apple Calendar sharing for training clients

Give clients an up-to-date view of their own sessions through an optional shared Apple Calendar.

A calendar for each client

TWORK can create individual client calendars for scheduled training. A client can follow the timetable in Apple Calendar instead of needing to learn the trainer’s entire CRM interface.

Relevant details, intentionally shared

Depending on the trainer’s Calendar Sync settings, calendar notes can contain package details, balances or coach comments. This makes sharing settings important: only share what the intended client should see.

Stay in control of permissions

Apple Calendar sync requires consent and uses Apple’s services. TWORK itself remains local-first; shared calendar data follows Apple account permissions and can be visible to people with access to the shared calendar.

Common questions

Must my client install TWORK?

For the Apple Calendar sharing workflow, the client can use Apple Calendar rather than the trainer app.

Can sensitive notes appear in a shared calendar?

Yes, depending on your synchronization settings. Review them before sharing; participants with calendar access may see the included details.
