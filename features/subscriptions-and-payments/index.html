TWORK FEATURES · iOS

Training packages, subscriptions and payment records

Follow prepaid sessions, package balances and trainer-side payment records without calculating everything in a spreadsheet.

Track what a client has purchased

Keep subscription records and the remaining training entitlement next to the client. Review how sessions affect the package and inspect related balance history when something does not add up.

Different services, different arrangements

TWORK supports single sessions, training packages and shared subscription workflows. The aim is to record the operational consequences of each training session, not to make you rebuild formulas.

Payment records are not a payment gateway

TWORK tracks debts, prepayments, adjustments and settlements for the trainer’s own bookkeeping. It is not presented as a card processor, bank account or accounting/tax filing service.

Common questions

Does TWORK charge clients' payment cards?

No card-payment gateway is advertised. TWORK records balances and payments entered in the app; an App Store subscription, if offered, is separate.

Can I review changes to a client balance?

The app has balance history and financial ledger workflows so a trainer can inspect the record behind a total.
