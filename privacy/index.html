Privacy Policy

Twork · Website analytics notice updated: October 29, 2026

Twork is a local-first application for trainers. We respect your privacy and are designed to keep your information under your control.

Data collection

The TWORK iOS app does not use an advertising or third-party tracking SDK and does not transmit its locally stored client records to the developer. Optional website analytics are described below and only enabled after consent.

Data stored on your device

Information you enter in Twork, such as client details, schedules, training records, subscriptions, notes, balances, services, app settings and related business information, is stored locally on your device.

iCloud and backups

If you choose to use iCloud backup or other Apple-provided storage features, copies of your Twork data may be stored in your personal iCloud account. Such storage and transmission are provided by Apple and are subject to Apple's terms and privacy policies. Twork does not operate its own cloud server for this data.

Calendar and Contacts

If you grant permission, Twork may access Apple Calendar to create and manage schedule events and may access Contacts when you choose to use contact information for client setup or messaging convenience. These permissions are used only to provide features you request.

Payments

Any financial or balance information entered in Twork is used for the trainer's own records and is not payment processing. App subscriptions and purchases, when applicable, are processed by Apple through the App Store.

Third-party content

Twork does not provide or distribute third-party media or other third-party content.

Deleting your data

You can delete Twork data from within the app. You may also remove the app from your device. Data copied to your personal iCloud account or Apple Calendar may need to be removed separately using the relevant Apple service.

Children

Twork is a professional productivity tool for trainers and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Requests for early access

If you send an early-access form, you choose to provide your name, email address, trainer category, and optionally a contact handle or comment. The form is submitted through the third-party service FormSubmit to the TWORK support inbox so that we can reply to your request. Please do not submit sensitive client information. Form submissions are separate from analytics events and are not used to identify people in GA4 or Clarity. You can contact twork.crm@gmail.com about your submission.

Website analytics and optional session recordings

The TWORK website is separate from the TWORK iOS application. If analytics are configured for this website, we ask for your consent before enabling third-party analytics scripts. Rejecting is just as easy as accepting, and the site remains accessible without optional tracking.

With your permission, Google Analytics 4 may process page views, country/device-level information, referring websites, campaign parameters, clicks on navigation and early-access buttons, scroll depth, and form-start/submission events. We do not deliberately send email addresses, phone numbers or the contents of signup fields as analytics events. Google may process IP data in transit and may use pseudonymous cookies to distinguish visits. We do not enable advertising personalization with our tag.

With a separate optional choice, Microsoft Clarity may record mouse/touch clicks, scrolling and interactions for heatmaps and session replay. Form fields and forms are masked; replay is not loaded unless you opt in. The service may create pseudonymous cookies and session identifiers when consent is given. We do not use these tools to establish your real-world identity.

Preferences are kept in your browser's localStorage. Use the Privacy settings control on this website to change or withdraw consent. Withdrawing optional tracking reloads the page without the analytics scripts. Removing your consent does not automatically delete data that providers already collected; contact us if you need a data-related request handled. Analytics and replay data are retained according to the configured provider settings and their applicable policies, and may be processed outside your country.

Providers: Google Privacy Policy and Microsoft Privacy Statement. Questions: twork.crm@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Twork's features or privacy practices change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
